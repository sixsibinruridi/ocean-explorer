HOW WE CAN HELP

Protecting Marine Ecosystems

Human activities such as plastic pollution, overfishing, and warming ocean temperatures put marine habitats at risk. Fortunately, collective action can help oceans recover.

  • Reduce Single-Use Plastics: Switch to reusable water bottles, bags, and containers to prevent ocean debris.
  • Support Sustainable Seafood: Choose responsibly harvested seafood to protect fish populations.
  • Join Coastal Cleanups: Participate in local beach or river cleanups to stop trash before it reaches the sea.
  • Conserve Energy: Lowering carbon emissions helps slow ocean warming and coral bleaching.
