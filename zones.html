LAYERS OF THE DEEP

OCEAN ZONES

Scientists divide the ocean into distinct vertical layers based on how much sunlight reaches each depth.

VERTICAL STRUCTURE

The Five Major Zones

As you descend deeper below the surface, light fades, temperatures drop, and water pressure increases dramatically.

  • Sunlight Zone (0 – 200m): Brightest layer where photosynthesis occurs and most marine life lives.
  • Twilight Zone (200 – 1,000m): Dim blue light where lanternfish and squid begin to use bioluminescence.
  • Midnight Zone (1,000 – 4,000m): Pitch-black waters with freezing temperatures and crushing pressure.
  • Abyssal Zone (4,000 – 6,000m): Near-freezing ocean floor inhabited by specialized deep-sea organisms.
  • Hadal Zone (6,000m+): Deepest ocean trenches, such as the Mariana Trench.
