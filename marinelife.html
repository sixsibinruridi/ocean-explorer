CREATURES OF THE SEA

Remarkable Ocean Species

Marine organisms have adapted to survive in every marine habitat on Earth.

  • Blue Whales: The largest animals ever known to have lived on Earth, reaching up to 30 meters long.
  • Sea Turtles: Ancient marine reptiles that travel thousands of miles across ocean basins to nest.
  • Coral Reefs: Often called the "rainforests of the sea," supporting 25% of all marine species.
  • Bioluminescent Jellyfish: Deep-sea drifters that produce their own light to deter predators.
