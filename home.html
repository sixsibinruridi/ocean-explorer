A World Beneath the Waves

The ocean is a vast interconnected body of salt water that covers more than 70% of Earth's surface. It is home to an incredible variety of life, from tiny plankton to enormous whales.

Beyond supporting marine biodiversity, the ocean regulates global temperatures, produces over half of the world's oxygen, and sustains coastal communities.

DID YOU KNOW?

Most of Earth's water is in the ocean.

About 97% of Earth's water is found in the ocean, while the remaining portion is distributed among glaciers, groundwater, lakes, rivers, and the atmosphere.
